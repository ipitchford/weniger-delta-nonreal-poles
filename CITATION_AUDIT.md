## Source-to-claim checks

* Borghi's question (§3.1) and the §3.3 sentences quoted in Section 1 were taken from the published article's text. The domain reviewer read the source in full and found the restatement faithful; the adversarial audit compared published equations (23) and (26) with the manuscript and confirmed that the manuscript's β is the published γ (the sentence saying so was added at revision).
* Homeier (2000) §6.3, Theorem 8 for accuracy through order: locator checked in the arXiv text (arXiv:math/0005209).
* Borghi–Weniger (2015) Theorem 5.1 is cited as quoted by Borghi (2026); a second auditor read the full-text negative-zero argument of its Section 5.2 during the novelty search.
* Szegő, Theorem 3.3.1 for the zeros of Laguerre polynomials (checked by the re-review); Weniger–Čížek (1990) for the K_ν moments; Borghi's arXiv:2605.06687 for the positive result on Γ(2ℓ + 1 + q). The re-review could not confirm that Slevinsky (2025) contains delta-transformation convergence results; the final text cites it in Section 1 only for its stable recurrences for Drummond and factorial Levin-type approximants and their pole placement.
* The re-review checked every quoted fragment of Borghi (2026) word for word against a freshly fetched copy of the published HTML, and found that Borghi had already proved several of the positive cases at n = 0, γ = 1 (§§4.2, 4.3, 4.6) and shown the non-integer Jacobi phenomenon (Fig. 1); the final text credits this.
* Craven–Csordas (1989), pp. 241–242: the Jensen-polynomial implication used in Corollary 6.2(3) was checked against the primary text by the second adversarial audit.
* Passare–Rojas–Shapiro: Theorem 1 and Corollary 1 were read; the manuscript's separating example (1, 8, 16, 8, 1) was checked algebraically by the adversarial audit.
